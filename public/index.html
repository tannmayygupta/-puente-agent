How it works

  1. You pay in dollars over x402. No account, no gas.
  2. Puente's agent waits until it sees your payment onchain, swaps it into pesos or reais on Textile FX, and sends them to the recipient.
  3. If anything fails, you are refunded automatically.

No custody pool

Each payment funds its own real Textile FX swap, then the pesos or reais go straight to the recipient.

Safe by design

Puente pays out only after it sees your payment onchain, and refunds automatically if anything fails.

Verifiable

ERC-8004 identity, tagged transactions and open stats. Check everything onchain.

For agents and developers

Any x402 client can use Puente. Create an order, then pay it. The 402 challenge lists the exact price.

curl -s -X POST {origin}/v1/orders -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"currency":"ARS","recipient":"0x…","usd":5}'
# then pay {origin}/v1/pay?order=<id> with @x402/fetch (USAT, USDT or USDC on Celo)
